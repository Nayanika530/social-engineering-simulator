How the Dual-System Pipeline Works

A modern offense vs. defense AI dynamic designed to understand attacker psychology and strengthen automated detection.

System 1: Attack Generator

Fine-tuned on 5,594 real phishing emails to generate scenario-specific social engineering attacks tailored dynamically by target role, organization, and context.

System 2: Threat Classifier

TF-IDF n-gram vectorizer paired with Logistic Regression trained on 17,537 curated samples. Achieves 97.75% test accuracy with probabilistic threat confidence scoring.

Real-Time Defense Dynamic

Connects offense and defense: evaluates how AI-generated spearphishing vectors interact with automated detection filters, highlighting vulnerabilities before adversaries exploit them.
